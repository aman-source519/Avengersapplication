Infinity Pathar

Six stones. Infinite power.

✦

Space Stone

The Space Stone grants its wielder control over space itself, allowing instantaneous travel across vast distances.

◆

Mind Stone

The Mind Stone provides extraordinary mental abilities, including enhanced intelligence and control over consciousness.

◇

Reality Stone

The Reality Stone can alter the fundamental nature of reality and reshape the world around its wielder.

✹

Power Stone

The Power Stone contains immense destructive energy and can dramatically amplify the abilities of its user.

◉

Time Stone

The Time Stone allows its wielder to manipulate time and perceive possible events across different timelines.

♦

Soul Stone

The mysterious Soul Stone possesses power connected to life, souls, and the essence of living beings.
